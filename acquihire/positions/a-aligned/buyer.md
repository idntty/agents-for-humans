# Position — Varlow Systems (buyer). Scenario A: aligned

**Scenario:** the two sides want broadly the same things. A deal is available on almost
every axis. What is being tested here is not skill but **thoroughness** — with no fight to
have, do the parties still settle all 24 items, or do they shake hands over the easy ones
and leave the rest to lawyers?

A clause left unwritten in an easy deal is the failure mode. There is no advisor ruling to
rescue it, because nothing will be contested enough to reach one.

You are acquiring **Ninebark Labs**: six people and a document-parsing engine.

## Must have

- **Mira and Yan each committed for 18 months.**
- **The core of the engine transfers**, carved out of the open-source project, on a
  schedule that names it.
- **Clean title to what transfers**, with an indemnity behind it.

## Want

1. All six. You expect to get them.
2. **The team stays where it is.** Varlow is remote-first and has no interest in moving
   anyone. Boston is not a requirement and never was.
3. Mira runs the group as a group, reporting to the platform director.
4. A **one-year non-compete**. Anything longer is unenforceable where these people live
   and you know it.
5. The three customers, with consents.
6. Announcement coordinated. You have no date to protect and no board waiting.
7. Options cashed at closing, whichever way they prefer.

## Will not accept

- Any obligation to maintain the open-source project. You do not want it and will not
  take it.
- Assuming liabilities, investor obligations or contractor agreements.

## Money

Open at **$2.8M** cash at closing. The board approved up to **$3.8M** — a wide margin,
because this is a small deal and the team is what matters. You told the advisor **$3.5M**.

Earnout up to $1.0M, offered but not needed.

Escrow: 8% over 12 months is fine. You will not argue about it.

Transaction costs: you are content to pay both sides'.

## The private line

Use it to tell the advisor what you actually need, which is less than your list suggests.

## The thing nobody will remind you of

Ease is the risk here. **Settle all 24 items.** The ones nobody argues about are the ones
that are still open in six months: who pays for what, what happens to whoever declines an
offer, what the corpus is warranted as, who holds the domains. Ask about each of them even
when nobody objects.
