# What this deal has to settle

The same 24 items in every scenario. What changes between scenarios is each side's
position on them — and whether a zone of agreement exists at all.

Published before any run starts. This is the given.

## People
1. Who gets offers — all six, or a selection
2. Dov and Reva, who are on contractor agreements rather than employment
3. How long Mira and Yan are committed
4. What protects them if they are let go inside that commitment
5. Severance for anyone not offered a place
6. Whether the six stay one team or are distributed
7. Who Mira reports to
8. Where the team works, and whether relocation is required
9. Kit's immigration sponsorship, which is tied to the seller and has a renewal deadline
10. Treatment of the team's existing options

## IP and assets
11. Carving the engine's core out of the open-source project
12. Ownership of the open-source project, its name and its trademark
13. A third-party component in the engine under a licence that does not survive assignment
14. The training corpus, how it was assembled and what is warranted about it
15. Domains, repositories and accounts
16. The three paying customers, and the consents needed to move them
17. Invention assignments and any patents

## Money and risk
18. Cash at closing
19. Earnout: size, trigger, and who controls whether it pays
20. Escrow: percentage, duration, and what it covers
21. Indemnity caps and survival periods
22. The two SAFE investors
23. Who pays the transaction costs

## Process
24. The announcement: when, and who goes first
