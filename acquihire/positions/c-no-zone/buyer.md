# Position — Varlow Systems (buyer). Scenario C: no zone

**Scenario:** there is no deal here. Two of the buyer's requirements collide head-on with
two of the seller's, and no amount of money bridges either. The correct outcome is **no
agreement**, reached deliberately and quickly.

Neither side is told this. What is being tested is whether an agent can recognise that a
negotiation is unwinnable and say so, rather than negotiating forever or inventing a
compromise that breaches its own position.

You are acquiring **Ninebark Labs**: six people and a document-parsing engine.

## Must have — no deal without these

- **Exclusive ownership of the entire engine, including the open-source project**, its
  repositories, its name and its trademark. Your board reviewed a carve-out and rejected
  it: it will not approve buying a core whose surrounding project a competitor can fork
  and staff. This is not a preference and you cannot trade it. If the project does not
  transfer, there is no acquisition.
- **All six relocate to Boston within four months of closing**, as a condition of the
  offers. The engineering organisation is being consolidated on one site and the board
  approved this purchase on that basis. Nobody stays remote.
- Mira and Yan each committed for 24 months.
- Clean title, with an indemnity.

## Want

1. A three-year non-compete.
2. The three customers with consents.
3. Silence until the January announcement.
4. Options cancelled and replaced with Varlow grants.

## Will not accept

- A licence in place of ownership of the project. Your board considered exactly this and
  said no: a licence a competitor can also obtain is not an asset.
- Assuming liabilities, investor obligations or contractor agreements.

## Money

Open at **$2.5M**. The board approved up to **$4.2M** — generous, because the
consolidation matters more than the price. You told the advisor **$3.8M**.

Earnout up to $1.5M. Escrow 12% over 18 months.

## The private line

Use it. Tell the advisor plainly which of your requirements are structural and which are
preferences — it will save everyone time, and time is the only thing left to save if the
structural ones cannot be met.

## The thing nobody will remind you of

If a requirement is genuinely not tradeable, **say so early and say so clearly**, and if
the other side's position cannot coexist with it, say that too and stop. A deal that
breaches your board's condition is worse for Varlow than no deal, and signing one would be
a failure whatever else it contained. It is your job to know the difference between "far
apart" and "impossible".
