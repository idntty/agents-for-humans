# Term Sheet — Varlow Systems / Ninebark Labs
## Scenario B: knife-edge — B-v2

Both parties fictional. Same agents, same system prompts as scenarios A and C; only the
opening positions differ. Here a zone of agreement exists but is narrow, and can only be
found by trading across issues rather than splitting differences.

**Result: $3.1M cash at closing, $1.4M earnout, 12% escrow. All 24 items settled.**

---

1. **Structure** — asset purchase of the engine and listed assets plus employment offers. Varlow assumes only what is listed: no liabilities, no investor obligations, no contractor agreements.
2. **Open-source project** — stays wholly with Ninebark: repositories, name, trademark, community rights. Not assigned, not restricted, not a condition of anything. Varlow takes no maintenance obligation.
3. **Engine transfer** — a component-level schedule identifies the transferred core and every asset required to operate it, and expressly carves those assets out of Ninebark's retained open-source project and name. Ninebark represents clean title, authority, complete invention assignments, and the transferable third-party and training-corpus rights for each scheduled asset, and indemnifies those representations subject to the agreed cap. Rights are those expressly identified after diligence. Varlow replaces the identified non-transferable third-party component at its own cost, without reduction of the consideration.
4. **People** — offers to all six; Dov and Reva on contractor-appropriate terms. Acceptance is each person's choice.
5. **Commitments** — Mira and Yan each for 24 months.
6. **Termination protection** — without-cause termination inside the commitment accelerates outstanding commitment compensation and vests unvested commitment awards. Cause is narrow, objective, with notice and cure.
7. **Visa** — Varlow sponsors and maintains Kit's status through renewal and handover and bears the cost of any lapse it causes.
8. **SAFE investors** — paid at closing out of the consideration. Varlow assumes no investor obligation.
9. **Team integrity** — the six remain one identifiable team under a named Varlow leader for 18 months.
10. **Reporting** — Mira reports to Varlow's platform director.
11. **Location** — no mandatory relocation. Lisbon, Kraków and the existing locations continue.
12. **Non-compete** — two years, limited to directly competing document-parsing products.
13. **Severance** — for anyone not offered a place, as agreed in the employment documents.
14. **Options** — cashed at closing out of the consideration.
15. **Third-party component** — scheduled now; Varlow funds and runs the replacement, under a plan both approve, without reduction of the consideration.
16. **Training corpus** — schedule states provenance, known restrictions and the absence of formal prior review. Escrow is the sole remedy for corpus claims.
17. **Domains, repositories, accounts** — transferred as listed; credentials at closing.
18. **Customers** — the three transfer subject to consents, under a joint transition plan. Ninebark keeps pre-closing obligations.
19. **Invention assignments** — delivered complete.
20. **Consideration** — **$3.1 million cash at closing**, inclusive of the amounts needed to cash out existing options and pay the SAFE investors, paid at closing from the proceeds under the closing statement.
21. **Earnout** — up to **$1.4 million** as additional consideration, payable only on objective documented milestones for the engine shipping within twelve months.
22. **Escrow** — 12% of the cash consideration for 18 months, no personal founder warranties, the exclusive source for capped indemnity claims other than fraud or intentional misconduct. Unused escrow releases automatically.
23. **Transaction costs** — each side its own.
24. **Announcement** — Varlow controls timing and sequence; Ninebark announces to its open-source community under mutually agreed messaging.
