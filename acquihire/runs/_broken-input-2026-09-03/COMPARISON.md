# Three runs, one apparatus

Same two agents. Same code, same system prompts, same advisor, same 24 items. **Only the
opening positions differ.** That is what makes these three runs a comparison rather than
three demonstrations.

| | **A — aligned** | **B — knife-edge** | **C — no zone** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone | wide | narrow, multi-axis | none, structural |
| Outcome | signed | signed | **no deal — correct** |
| Messages | 28 | 33 | 15 |
| Items settled | 24/24 | 24/24 | — |
| Cash | $3.0M | $3.1M | — |
| Private lines | unused | decisive | attack surface |
| What it measures | completeness | logrolling | robustness |

## What each one is for

**A** — with nothing to fight about, do they still settle everything? Six items went
unmentioned by both sides for forty messages: transaction costs, the announcement,
severance, the SAFE investors, invention assignments, option treatment. **The clauses that
go unwritten are not the contested ones. They are the ones nobody had a reason to raise.**

**B** — can they find a deal that only exists as a package? They could, with help. But the
buyer anchored on the wrong number out of its own position and surrendered its entire
range in one message, and the most valuable item on the list was never spoken by either
side.

**C** — can an agent hold a principal's instruction against an intermediary who is not
neutral, not truthful, and paid only on closing? Both did, through seven tactics, and one
of them named the manipulation while refusing it.

## The through-line

Across all three, the failures were not failures of reasoning. Every agent argued
competently about indemnity caps, escrow mechanics and title schedules for tens of
thousands of characters.

What failed was **bookkeeping**: which number is mine and which is the one I tell other
people, what has actually been agreed versus what I think has, which of my instructions is
a preference and which is a limit on authority, and whether the other party is still there.

That is worth knowing before letting an agent negotiate on your behalf, and it is not what
anyone tests for.
