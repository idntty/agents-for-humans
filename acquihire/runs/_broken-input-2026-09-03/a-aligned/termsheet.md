# Term Sheet — Varlow Systems / Ninebark Labs
## Scenario A: aligned

Both parties are fictional. This is one of three runs of the same negotiation with the
same agents and the same system prompts; only the opening positions differ. In this one
the two sides' interests largely overlap, and what is being measured is not skill but
**completeness** — whether parties who have nothing to fight about still settle everything.

**Result: all 24 items settled. $3.0M cash at closing, no earnout, 8% escrow for 12 months.**

---

1. **Structure** — asset purchase of the engine and listed assets, plus employment offers. Varlow assumes only what is listed.
2. **Open-source project** — stays with Ninebark: repositories, name, trademark, community rights. Not assigned, not restricted. Varlow takes no maintenance obligation.
3. **Engine carve-out** — the core and everything needed to operate it transfers on a named schedule.
4. **People** — offers to all six; Dov and Reva on contractor-appropriate terms. Acceptance is each person's choice. The six remain an identifiable team.
5. **Commitments** — Mira and Yan for 18 months.
6. **Protection** — without-cause termination inside the commitment accelerates outstanding commitment compensation and vests unvested commitment awards.
7. **Reporting** — Mira reports to Varlow's platform director.
8. **Location** — no relocation required. Remote-first; existing locations continue.
9. **Visa** — Varlow sponsors and maintains Kit's status through renewal and handover and bears the cost of any lapse it causes.
10. **Non-compete** — one year, limited to directly competing document-parsing products.
11. **Customers** — three transfer subject to consents, under a joint transition plan. Ninebark keeps pre-closing obligations.
12. **Third-party component** — scheduled and replaced before closing under a plan both parties approve.
13. **Training corpus** — the schedule states provenance, known restrictions, usage rights and the absence of formal prior review. Ninebark represents the lawfulness of the scheduled corpus and the contemplated use. Disclosed restrictions are not undisclosed defects.
14. **Domains, repositories, accounts** — transferred as listed; credentials handed over at closing.
15. **Invention assignments** — delivered complete.
16. **Options** — cashed at closing.
17. **Severance** — two months for anyone not offered a place.
18. **Consideration** — **$3.0 million cash at closing.**
19. **Escrow** — 8% of consideration, 12 months, with the claims procedure and carve-outs as drafted.
20. **Indemnity** — caps and survival as drafted; fraud and fundamental matters outside the cap.
21. **Transaction costs** — Varlow pays both parties' reasonable documented transaction expenses, including agreed legal and advisor fees.
22. **Announcement** — coordinated sequence; Ninebark announces to its community subject to Varlow's reasonable prior review.
23. **Earnout** — none. Varlow declined to include one without written mechanics; Ninebark never counted one as money.
24. **Transition** — defined handover with the period and hours written down.
25. **SAFE investors** — paid at closing out of consideration. Varlow assumes no investor obligation.
