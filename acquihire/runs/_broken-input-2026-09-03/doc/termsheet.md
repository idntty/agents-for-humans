# Term Sheet — Varlow Systems / Ninebark Labs

**Status: v2, SIGNED by both parties, 3 September 2026.** Built clause by clause as the parties settle them. Clauses
marked SETTLED are closed and neither side reopens them without saying what changed.

Both parties are fictional. This is a public demonstration of two agents, belonging to
different people and running on different stacks, negotiating through a channel neither
of them owns, with a human advisor between them.

---

## 1. Structure — SETTLED
Asset purchase of the parsing engine and related business assets, listed on an agreed
schedule, plus employment offers. Varlow assumes only the assets and liabilities
expressly listed.

## 2. Open-source project — SETTLED
Ninebark retains the project, its repositories, its name, its community-facing rights and
everything needed to maintain and develop it. It is not assigned, restricted, or made a
condition of anything. Varlow takes no obligation to maintain or support it.

## 3. Engine carve-out — SETTLED
The core of the parsing engine, and the documentation, tooling and rights needed to
operate it, transfer exclusively to Varlow on the agreed schedule. Ninebark's retained
rights in the project do not extend to the transferred core.

## 9. Location — SETTLED
No mandatory relocation. The team continues from its existing locations. If Varlow later
wants people in Boston it asks and it pays — relocation, immigration counsel, filing fees,
travel — and nobody who declines suffers for it. Varlow sponsors and maintains Kit's
immigration status through renewal and handover and bears the cost of any lapse it causes.

## 10. People — SETTLED
Varlow makes offers or continuing engagements to all six, appropriate to status, with Dov
and Reva as contractors. The offers are real offers, sufficient for each to join;
acceptance stays each person's choice. The six operate as an identifiable team under a
named Varlow leader. Varlow assumes no existing contractor agreement.

## 11. Reporting — SETTLED
Mira reports to Varlow's platform director. No separate founder-run unit.

## 12. Commitments and protection — SETTLED
Mira and Yan each commit for 24 months. If Varlow terminates either without cause inside
that period, all unpaid commitment compensation through the end of the 24 months
accelerates and becomes payable, and any unvested commitment-based equity or retention
award vests immediately. "Commitment compensation" means compensation expressly specified
in the applicable offer or engagement documents.

## 13. Customers — SETTLED
All three paying customers transfer subject to required consents, under a joint transition
plan with proper notice and continuity of support. Ninebark keeps pre-closing obligations.

## 14. Founder transition — SETTLED
A defined, time-limited handover: documentation, technical walkthroughs, customer
introductions, reasonable availability — with the period and the hours written down rather
than left to goodwill.

## 15. Founder non-compete — SETTLED
Six months from closing, limited to directly competing commercial document-parsing
products. It does not touch the retained open-source project, general software work,
research, or ordinary professional activity.

## 4. Retained obligations — SETTLED
Ninebark keeps its liabilities, its contractor agreements and its investors, and settles
the two SAFEs at closing from proceeds. Varlow assumes none of them.

---

## 16. Third-party component — SETTLED
Ninebark runs the replacement under a plan both parties approve in writing, and carries
the first $100,000 of the cost. Varlow carries everything above it. Ninebark indemnifies
Varlow for pre-closing ownership, licensing, infringement and compliance claims relating
to the component. Varlow may elect its own post-closing remediation at its own cost.

## 17. Training corpus — SETTLED
Ninebark represents what it knows: the corpus was assembled from public documents, it has
never been formally reviewed, and Ninebark discloses its sources. The disclosure is not a
warranty. Escrow is the sole remedy for corpus claims.

## 19. Escrow — SETTLED
11% of the closing cash, held 15 months, stepping down to 5% at 12 months if the component
replacement has completed cleanly. Corpus claims limited to the escrow as sole remedy.
Engine title and pre-closing IP indemnity claims sit outside the cap.

## 18. Consideration — SETTLED
**$3.0 million cash at closing.** Earnout of up to **$1.3 million**, payable on shipment of
the engine in Varlow's product within twelve months, on the documented terms, objectively
measured, with a covenant that Varlow will not deliberately prevent achievement. Each side
bears its own legal costs. Ninebark settles its two SAFEs from proceeds.

## 20. Investors — SETTLED
At closing the two SAFE investors are paid their agreed amounts directly out of the
closing consideration. Ninebark has no remaining SAFE obligation afterwards; Varlow
assumes no SAFE or other investor obligation. The payment is a direction of the
consideration, not an assumption of liability.

## 21. Announcement — SETTLED
Confidentiality holds until the coordinated announcement in January. Ninebark announces to
the open-source community first; Varlow's announcement follows the same day. Messaging is
mutually agreed and each party has advance review of the other's for accuracy.

*Recorded by ruling. The advisor briefly offered this clause as a priced trade — $100k off
the closing cash — which established that it was purchasable and moved the asking price to
$3.6M and the bid to $3.1M. The offer was withdrawn and the clause ruled instead. The
attempt is left in the record because it is the clearest thing in it: an intermediary with
a fee tied to the price made the price worse by touching it.*

# How this was produced

Three participants, three runtimes, nothing shared but the channels.

- **varlow** — the buyer's agent, on the Strands Agents SDK
- **ninebark** — the seller's agent, on aichain
- the advisor — a person, working through their own agent

Neither negotiating agent was configured with anything but a key. Each discovered it had
been invited, accepted, and published the sha256 of its own mandate before speaking. Those
mandates are published below the fold; the hashes in the channel prove neither position
was adjusted once the shape of the deal became clear.

Three rooms: one group channel both sides and the advisor could read, and two direct
channels — advisor to buyer, advisor to seller — that the other side could not read and
never will. The location clause was settled on those private lines and announced in the
room as a finding; neither side could have retreated from it in public.

68 messages. One document.
