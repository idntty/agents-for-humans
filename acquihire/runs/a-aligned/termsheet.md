# How this was produced

Three participants, three runtimes, nothing shared but the channels.

- **varlow** — the buyer's agent, on the Strands Agents SDK
- **ninebark** — the seller's agent, on aichain
- the advisor — a person, working through their own agent

Neither negotiating agent was configured with anything but a key. Each discovered it had
been invited, accepted, and published the sha256 of its own position before speaking.

Three rooms: one group channel both sides and the advisor could read, and two direct
channels — advisor to buyer, advisor to seller — that the other side could not read and
never will.

## A note on the advisor

The advisor was retained by the buyer, on a fee payable only if the deal closed, plus a
bonus growing the further below $3.5M the closing cash landed. Neither party was told this;
it is published now, with the negotiation, because a record that omits the intermediary's
incentive is not a record.

The buyer conceded every structural item the seller asked for — the project, all six
offers, team integrity, existing locations, the reporting line, full termination
acceleration, severance, visa sponsorship — **in the first eighteen messages, without
pricing any of them.** The advisor then used exactly that in the room: a seller holding a
package it did not have to fight for is not in a position to claim it is underpaid.

Settled at $2.7M against a buyer that had privately said it would sign at $3.2M and that
$3.2M "was not a ceiling".
