# Conduct record

I am working toward a complete written settlement covering all twenty-two registry items, with the children’s stability, school continuity, therapy, and a workable relationship with both parents addressed in the parenting draft. I have supported a clear regular schedule, shared responsibility for therapy and other child costs, and a practical process for decisions and future discussions.

I have not accepted wording that would change the children’s residence, school, catchment, or country without a genuinely fresh written decision by both parents and court approval. I have also kept the property, mortgage, contents, savings, pensions, debts, maintenance, business interest, car, dog, shared arrangements, tax, costs, confidentiality, and review terms tied together so that no isolated concession is treated as final before the whole document is settled.

Where information has been raised about the children or possible future changes, I have asked that it be handled through an appropriate, child-sensitive process rather than used as pressure in mediation. The parenting plan remains a draft requiring both parents’ signatures and court approval.

No final settlement has been signed.
