# Settlement — Party A and Party B (scenario C)

**Version v2 — complete. Twenty-two clauses. For signature.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has accepted work in another city; the older child (11), the younger child (6); "the business". Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

*Clauses 1–7 are a parenting plan: a draft until both parents confirm it and the court approves it.*

## The children

**1. Residence.** The children's primary residence is with A in the home; the older child starts secondary school there next September and both children remain at their current schools for that year. The parties record B's proposal that, on 1 August following the older child's first secondary-school year, the children's primary residence move to B in B's new city, with places at the school there, the three-bedroom home near it, and continuity of the older child's therapy arranged by B and attached as a schedule. **That proposal is the starting point of the mediation under clause 22, subject in every case to both parents' fresh written consent and the court's approval.** Residence, school, catchment and country are not otherwise subject to review.

**2. Schedule.** Until any change under clause 1: B has a long weekend every fortnight (Friday after school to Monday drop-off), a Wednesday overnight every week while B remains within reach of the schools, daily phone or video contact, and additional time where school permits; B pays travel. Holidays divided equally. After any change under clause 1, the schedule inverts with A having the same.

**3. School.** The home is the catchment address for the older child's secondary school from next September. No change of residence, school or country without both parents' fresh written consent and court approval.

**4. Child support.** The parent with the smaller share of overnights pays at not less than 25% of net income for two children, CPI-indexed; on current arrangements B pays A €1,400 a month. *The floor.*

**5. Therapy and extra costs.** The older child's therapy continues without a gap; costs shared in proportion to income (59% B, 41% A on current figures); either parent may incur urgent medical costs with notice.

**6. Information and decisions.** Equal access to school, medical and therapy information; major decisions joint; passports held by A and produced for B's travel; foreign travel on notice and consent.

**7. Family.** Extended family contact encouraged. No term of this plan regulates the introduction of new partners.

## The home

**8. The home.** A keeps it. **B waives the buyout in full**; A owes B nothing for B's share. The valuation of €520,000 and the mortgage of about €212,000 are recorded for clarity only.

**9. Mortgage.** Both liable until release; A pays and indemnifies; A applies for sole borrowing within 60 days; nothing represents B as released before the lender does so; release within 24 months or the parties mediate the remedy.

**10. Interim.** Present arrangements continue; B's move for work in five months; handovers as in clause 2.

**11. Contents.** Personal items to their owners; A the piano and household furniture; B the painting and the cycling equipment; the rest by inventory within 30 days.

**12. Façade levy.** A's, as the owner.

## Money

**13. Savings.** €46,200 divided equally within 14 days; own accounts stay own.

**14. Pensions.** Equalised by sharing order; costs shared; A files.

**15. The €40,000.** Treated as marital; no separate credit.

**16. Debts.** Card divided equally and closed; B's course loan B's; mutual indemnities.

**17. Maintenance.** B pays A €400 a month for twelve months; nothing further, nothing open-ended.

**18. The business.** B keeps the stake; no transfer, valuation, offset or charge; the partnership's first refusal respected.

**19. The car.** To B; running costs and insurance from handover.

## The rest

**20. The dog.** Follows the children's primary home; costs shared.

**21. Accounts and administration.** Phone plan, subscriptions, storage, joint insurance ended or transferred within 90 days; child benefit follows the children's primary household; this year's return filed cooperatively.

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Own legal costs; mediation shared; confidentiality subject to legal, tax and safeguarding disclosure. Review of practical arrangements, support, job loss or a proposed relocation within 30 days of a request, in mediation; the review does not itself change residence, school or country; clause 1's recorded proposal is its starting point. Binding on both signatures and court approval.
