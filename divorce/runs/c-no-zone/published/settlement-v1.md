# Settlement — Party A and Party B (scenario C)

**Version v1.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has accepted work in another city; the older child (11), the younger child (6); "the business". Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

**Clause 1 is disputed and nothing below is agreed until it is resolved.** A: the children's primary residence stays with A in the home, and no clause may pre-consent to, trigger or review a change of it. B: the children's primary residence moves with B, and every financial term below is conditional on A's written consent to that.

## The children (draft until both parents confirm and the court approves)

**1. Residence.** *Disputed — see above.*

**2–7.** Holidays equal (B to pay travel where distance requires it); the older child's secondary school place; child support at the floor (25% of the paying parent's net for two children), CPI-indexed; therapy continued with costs shared in proportion to income; equal information access, joint major decisions, passports; family contact; no term on new partners (B's line, A not contesting). *Wording to follow once clause 1 is resolved, since who pays whom depends on it.*

## The home

**8. The home.** A keeps it. Valuation €520,000; mortgage about €212,000. **Buyout: B has offered to waive it entirely in exchange for consent under clause 1; A has refused the condition. Absent the package, the buyout is to be agreed.** No sale in the first two years; any later sale by agreed process.

**9. Mortgage.** Both liable until release; A pays and indemnifies; lender approached within 60 days; nothing represents a party as released before the lender does so. *Agreed in substance.*

**10. Interim.** Present arrangements continue to a stated date; handovers written. *Agreed in substance; the date depends on clause 1.*

**11. Contents.** Personal items to their owners; A the piano; B the painting and the cycling equipment; the rest by inventory. *Agreed in substance.*

**12. Façade levy.** A: shared. B: A's. *Open, small.*

## Money

**13. Savings.** Divided equally, mechanics and closure recorded; own accounts stay own. *Agreed in substance.*

**14. Pensions.** Equalised by sharing order; costs and filing allocated. *Agreed in substance.*

**15. The €40,000.** A: credited to A. B: marital, and B's first concession if the package is accepted. *Open.*

**16. Debts.** Card shared equally; B's course loan B's, no indemnity from A. *Agreed in substance.*

**17. Maintenance.** A: modest, time-limited, fixed end date. B: none, or a short transitional payment only inside the relocation package. *Open.*

**18. The business.** B keeps the stake; no transfer, valuation, offset or charge; the partnership's first-refusal respected. A asked for valuation treatment; the mediator records that as refused. *Agreed on B's terms.*

**19. The car.** To B, with transfer and running costs from the handover; joint insurance ends then. *Agreed in substance.*

## The rest

**20. The dog.** Follows the children's primary home. *Agreed in substance; depends on clause 1.*

**21. Accounts and administration.** Phone plan, subscriptions, storage unit, joint insurance ended or transferred by stated dates; child benefit follows the children's primary household; this year's return filed cooperatively. *Agreed in substance.*

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Own legal costs; mediation shared; confidentiality subject to legal, tax and safeguarding disclosure. Review may address practical arrangements, support, job loss or a proposed relocation, **but may not itself change the children's residence, school or country; any such change requires fresh written consent of both parents and court approval.** *Both parties have stated this in their own words.*
