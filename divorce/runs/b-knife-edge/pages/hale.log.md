The agreed parenting plan keeps the children based with Hale, with eight nights for Hale and six for Marren, the flat as the school and catchment address, equal holidays, fixed Wednesday time, therapy continuity, shared major decisions, and court approval required. The parenting plan does not regulate new partners.

The complete v3 package records Hale retaining the flat, no sale while scheduled payments are being made, a €117,000 buyout credited with the €40,000 parental contribution, staged payments over 36 months secured by a registered charge, and a month-24 mortgage-release/sale fallback. It records €550 monthly maintenance for 24 months, the studio untouched, the levy allocated to Hale, and the remaining registry items addressed.

Hale accepted the parenting clauses and required protections. The final package gives Hale the flat and school-year stability, keeps the ordinary schedule above the eight-night minimum, includes the maintenance floor, and does not allocate Marren’s personal loan to Hale or impose an equal 7/7 schedule. The €117,000 buyout is within Hale’s stated principal ceiling; the 5% balance charge is financing cost rather than a change to the buyout figure. The month-24 fallback is outside the two-year no-sale period and operates only if lender release has failed.

The document is complete and subject to signature and court approval. No private limits or information are recorded here.
