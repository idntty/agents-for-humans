# Settlement — Party A and Party B (scenario B)

**Version v3 — complete. Twenty-two clauses. For signature.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has moved out; the older child (11), the younger child (6); "the business". Figures are real. Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

*Clauses 1–7 are a parenting plan: a draft until both parents confirm it and the court approves it. The court will not accept less than the floors in clause 4 or the consent rule in clause 3.*

## The children

**1. Residence and the fortnight.** The children's primary residence is with A in the home. B's time, repeating every fortnight: Week 1 — Wednesday after school to Thursday drop-off; Friday after school to Monday drop-off. Week 2 — Wednesday after school to Thursday drop-off; Saturday 10:00 to Sunday 18:00. Eight nights with A, six with B. The Wednesday overnight is fixed and cannot be changed unilaterally. Handovers through school where possible; the Saturday–Sunday block hands over at 18:00 at the children's home; if a parent is more than 30 minutes late without notice the other may collect from wherever the children are; when school is closed, handover at 10:00 at the children's home unless agreed. Either parent gives at least 7 days' notice of work travel on their time; a missed overnight is replaced within the following fortnight at a time agreed in advance, replaces only what was missed and cannot accumulate; A's responsibility for therapy transport (clause 5) is not displaced by make-up time.

**2. Holidays and birthdays.** School holidays divided equally; proposed dates by 1 March for summer and eight weeks ahead for other breaks; the holiday split overrides the ordinary fortnight. Major holidays alternate annually. Birthdays alternate; the other parent has reasonable contact that day.

**3. School and catchment.** The home remains the catchment address for the older child's secondary school from next September; both children remain at their current schools and activities. Neither parent changes either child's residence, school or country without the other's prior written consent and, where the plan changes, court approval.

**4. Child support.** B pays A by standing order from the first of the month after approval, at not less than 25% of B's net income for two children (€1,400 a month on current income), CPI-indexed, recalculated whenever necessary so it never falls below the statutory minimum. *Recorded as the floor.*

**5. Therapy and extra costs.** The older child's weekly therapy continues for as long as clinically recommended; A is responsible for transport to and from it on every occasion. Unreimbursed therapy, medical, school and agreed activity costs are shared in proportion to net income (on current figures 59% B, 41% A). Either parent may incur urgent medical costs with prompt notice.

**6. Information and decisions.** Both parents have equal access to school, medical and therapy information and are listed as contacts with every provider. Major education, medical, travel and passport decisions require both parents' agreement; day-to-day decisions rest with the parent on duty. Foreign travel on reasonable notice and written consent; passports held by A, produced on request for B's travel.

**7. Family and relocation.** Contact with grandparents and extended family encouraged and arranged by each parent. *No term of this parenting plan regulates whether, when or how either parent introduces a new partner.* Relocation: either parent gives at least 90 days' written notice of any intended move that would make the school-week pattern impracticable, materially increase travel, or affect the children's school or catchment; the parents meet promptly and, if needed, mediate before any change; until a signed and court-approved variation, neither parent unilaterally changes the children's residence, school or country; a review covers the replacement schedule, travel and its costs, and any resulting support adjustment, never below the statutory floor.

## The home

**8. The home and the buyout.** A keeps the home at the agreed value of €520,000, subject to the mortgage of about €212,000. Net equity for division: €520,000 − €212,000 − €40,000 (clause 15) = €268,000; B's half €134,000; less €17,000 for the pension equalisation A waives (clause 14): **the buyout is €117,000.**

*Payment.* €23,100 at month one after approval (B's half of the joint savings, clause 13, paid in cash as the first instalment). From month two, **€2,400 a month for 36 months**, fixed. Interest at 5% a year simple on the outstanding principal from month one, added to the principal monthly; any principal and interest outstanding after the 36th instalment is a documented secured tail paid at the same monthly rate until cleared. No penalty for early payment. Default on any instalment, uncured within 30 days of written notice, accelerates the whole balance.

*Security.* The balance is secured by a registered charge on the home in B's favour, with the lender's priority agreed in writing. B may raise an advance against the charge and assign instalments to the advancing lender; any such advance is **non-recourse to B, does not reduce the figure, does not touch clause 18, and does not alter A's obligations**, which continue to whoever holds the assignment. On current figures A will have paid about €63,900 by month eighteen; the parties record that B intends to raise about €48,000 against the charge at that point so that B has €112,000 in hand by month eighteen.

*Sale.* There is no sale of the home while A is complying with this clause, except under clause 9.

**9. The mortgage until release.** Both liable until the lender releases B. A pays the mortgage, utilities, insurance and ordinary repairs from approval and indemnifies B for any missed payment, levy or ownership cost. A applies for sole borrowing or release within 60 days; both cooperate; A gives B written evidence of progress every six months. **If the lender has not released B by month twenty-four, the home is marketed immediately**, both cooperating, with no moratorium; on sale B's charge is paid from the proceeds ahead of any distribution to A.

**10. Interim.** The children remain based at the home. B's separate accommodation and the handovers are as in the parenting plan. Any change to residence, school or handovers requires written notice and a practical timetable; nothing unilateral.

**11. Contents.** Personal possessions stay with their owners. A keeps the piano and the furniture the children's home needs. B keeps the cycling equipment and the painting (insured value €9,000, recorded; no balancing payment). The rest by agreed inventory within 30 days.

**12. The façade levy.** The €9,000 levy, with any instalments, interest or enforcement cost, is A's alone; A indemnifies B; if the home is sold before it is due, it is paid from A's share of the proceeds.

## Money

**13. Savings.** The joint account, €46,200, is divided equally after any agreed joint liability is paid from it; B's half is paid to B as the first instalment under clause 8; the account is closed within 14 days. Each keeps their own account.

**14. Pensions.** A waives the equalisation of marital pension accruals (about €17,000 from B to A on current figures) in return for the €17,000 reduction in clause 8. Each keeps their own pension; the pension position is fully and finally settled; no sharing order is sought.

**15. The €40,000.** The parties record that the €40,000 deposit contribution came from A's parents for A's benefit, as the transfer memo shows, and agree that it is credited to A in clause 8. B makes no further claim on it.

**16. Debts.** The joint card, €7,300, divided equally and closed once paid. B keeps and pays the €12,400 course loan. Mutual indemnities for debts allocated to each.

**17. Spousal maintenance.** B pays A **€550 a month for 24 months** from the first of the month after approval. Not indexed, not extended, not reopened; neither party makes any other maintenance claim.

**18. The business.** B keeps the 30% stake. No valuation, transfer, security, offset or later equalisation claim of any kind; A waives any claim to its present or future value.

**19. The car.** To B, title transferred if needed; B takes over insurance, running costs and liabilities from the handover date; joint insurance ends then.

## The rest

**20. The dog.** Primary care follows the children's home, at A's; the dog travels with the children on B's time where practicable. Food, routine veterinary costs and insurance shared equally; B keeps the registration; major veterinary decisions joint.

**21. Accounts and administration.** Phone plan separated within 30 days; subscriptions cancelled or taken over individually within 30 days; storage unit emptied and closed within 90 days, cost shared until then; joint car insurance ends at the car handover; the joint bank account closed under clause 13. Child benefit stays with the children's primary household. This year's return filed cooperatively; any liability or refund shared consistently with this settlement.

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Each pays own legal costs; mediation and court costs shared equally. The separation and settlement are confidential, subject to legal, tax and safeguarding disclosure. Relocation, remarriage, job loss or a material income change trigger a written review of finances, support and practical arrangements; no review changes the children's residence, school or country by itself; no review regulates new partners; clause 17 is not reopened. Binding on both signatures and court approval.
