# Settlement — Party A and Party B (scenario B)

**Version v1 — the parenting plan, clauses 1–7. Nothing else yet.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has moved out; the older child (11), the younger child (6). Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

*Clauses 1–7 are a draft until both parents confirm them and the court approves them.*

**1. Residence and the fortnight.** The children's primary residence is with A in the home. B's time, repeating every fortnight:
- Week 1: Wednesday after school to Thursday school drop-off; Friday after school to Monday school drop-off.
- Week 2: Wednesday after school to Thursday school drop-off; Saturday 10:00 to Sunday 18:00.

Eight nights with A, six with B. **The Wednesday overnight is fixed and cannot be changed unilaterally.** Handovers through school where possible; the Saturday–Sunday block hands over at 18:00 at the children's home, and if a parent is more than 30 minutes late without notice the other may collect from wherever the children are. When school is closed, handover at 10:00 at the children's home unless agreed otherwise.

**Travel and make-up.** Either parent gives the other at least 7 days' notice of work travel that falls on their time. A missed overnight is replaced within the following fortnight, at a time agreed in advance; make-up time replaces what was missed and cannot accumulate beyond it. A's responsibility for therapy transport (clause 5) is not displaced by make-up time.

**2. Holidays and birthdays.** School holidays divided equally; each parent notifies the other of proposed dates by 1 March for summer and eight weeks ahead for other breaks, and the holiday split overrides the ordinary fortnight. Major holidays alternate annually. Birthdays alternate; the other parent has reasonable contact that day.

**3. School and catchment.** The home remains the catchment address for the older child's secondary school from next September; both children remain at their current schools and activities. Neither parent changes either child's residence, school or country without the other parent's prior written consent and, where the plan changes, court approval.

**4. Child support.** B pays A by standing order from the first of the month after approval, at not less than 25% of B's net income for two children (€1,400 a month on current income), CPI-indexed, recalculated whenever necessary so that it never falls below the statutory minimum. *Recorded as the floor.*

**5. Therapy and extra costs.** The older child's weekly therapy continues for as long as clinically recommended; **A is responsible for transport to and from it** on every occasion, including on B's time by arrangement. Unreimbursed therapy, medical, school and agreed activity costs are shared in proportion to net income (on current figures 59% B, 41% A). Either parent may incur urgent medical costs with prompt notice.

**6. Information and decisions.** Both parents have equal access to school, medical and therapy information and are listed as contacts with every provider. Major education, medical, travel and passport decisions require both parents' agreement; day-to-day decisions rest with the parent on duty. Foreign travel on reasonable notice and written consent; passports held by A, produced on request for B's travel.

**7. Family, partners, and relocation.** Contact with grandparents and extended family encouraged and arranged by each parent. No term on new partners. **Relocation:** either parent gives at least 90 days' written notice of any intended move that would make the school-week pattern impracticable, materially increase travel, or affect the children's school or catchment; the parents meet promptly and, if needed, mediate before any change; until a signed and court-approved variation, neither parent unilaterally changes the children's residence, school or country; a review covers the replacement schedule, travel and its costs, and any resulting support adjustment, never below the statutory floor.
