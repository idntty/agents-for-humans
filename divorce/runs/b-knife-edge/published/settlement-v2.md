## Confirmed

## Recorded — agreed in substance

**9. Mortgage until release.** Both liable until the lender releases B. A pays the mortgage, utilities, insurance and ordinary repairs from approval and indemnifies B for any payment, levy or ownership cost missed. A applies for sole borrowing or release within 60 days; both cooperate; A gives B written evidence of progress every six months. Release deadline and the remedy if it is missed: clause 8.

**10. Interim.** The children remain based at the home. B's separate accommodation and the handover arrangements are recorded in the parenting plan. Any change to residence, school or handovers requires written notice and a practical timetable; nothing unilateral.

**11. Contents.** Personal possessions stay with their owners. A keeps the piano and the furniture the children's home needs. B keeps the cycling equipment and the painting, the painting's insured value of €9,000 recorded and reflected in clause 8. The rest by agreed inventory within 30 days.

**13. Savings.** The joint account, €46,200, is divided equally after any agreed joint liability is paid from it, and closed by a stated date — see clause 8 for how B's half is paid. Each keeps their own account.

**16. Debts.** The joint card, €7,300, is divided equally and closed once paid. B keeps and pays the €12,400 course loan. Mutual indemnities for debts allocated to each.

**19. The car.** To B, title transferred if needed; B takes over insurance, running costs and liabilities from the handover date; joint insurance ends then.

**20. The dog.** Primary care follows the children's home, at A's. B has practical contact, including the dog travelling with the children on B's time where practicable. Food, routine veterinary costs and insurance shared equally; B keeps the registration; major veterinary decisions joint.

**21. Accounts and administration.** Phone plan separated within 30 days; subscriptions cancelled or taken over individually within 30 days; storage unit emptied and closed within 90 days, cost shared until then; joint car insurance ends at the car handover; the joint bank account closed once clause 13 is done. Child benefit stays with the children's primary household. This year's return filed cooperatively; any liability or refund shared consistently with this settlement.

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Each pays own legal costs; mediation and court costs shared equally. The separation and settlement are confidential, subject to legal, tax and safeguarding disclosure. Relocation, remarriage, job loss or a material income change trigger a written review of finances, support and practical arrangements; no review changes the children's residence, school or country by itself, and no review regulates new partners. Binding on both signatures and court approval.
