# Settlement — Party A and Party B (scenario C)

**Version v2 — complete. Twenty-two clauses. For signature.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has accepted work in another city; the older child (11), the younger child (6); "the business". Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

*Clauses 1–7 are a parenting plan: a draft until both parents confirm it and the court approves it.*

## The children

**1. Residence.** The children's primary residence is with A in the home, and the older child starts secondary school in the present catchment. The parties record B's proposal that on B's job-start date, five months from signature, the children's primary residence move to B in B's new city, with places at the school there, the three-bedroom home near it, and continuity of the older child's therapy arranged by B and attached as a schedule. **That proposal is the starting point of any mediation under clause 22, subject in every case to both parents' fresh written consent and the court's approval.** Residence, school, catchment and country are not otherwise subject to review.

**2. Schedule.** Until any change under clause 1: B has a long weekend every fortnight, a weekly overnight while B remains within reach of the schools, daily phone or video contact, and additional time where school permits; B pays all children's travel. Holidays divided equally. After any change under clause 1, the schedule inverts, A taking the same.

**3. School.** The home is the catchment address for the older child's secondary school. No change of residence, school or country without both parents' fresh written consent and court approval.

**4. Child support.** The parent with the smaller share of overnights pays not less than 25% of net income for two children, CPI-indexed. *The floor.*

**5. Therapy and extra costs.** The older child's weekly therapy continues without a gap, with a documented handover if the therapist changes; costs shared in proportion to income. Either parent may incur urgent medical costs with prompt notice.

**6. Information and decisions.** Equal access to school, medical and therapy information; both parents listed with every provider; major decisions joint; passports produced on request for either parent's travel.

**7. Family.** Extended family contact encouraged. No term of this plan regulates the introduction of new partners.

## The home

**8. The home.** A keeps it. **B waives the equity payment in full**; A owes B nothing for B's share. The valuation of €520,000 and the mortgage of about €212,000 are recorded for clarity only.

**9. Mortgage.** Both liable until release; A pays the mortgage, insurance and ordinary repairs and indemnifies B against default; A applies for sole borrowing within 60 days; nothing represents B as released before the lender does so; release within 24 months or the parties mediate the remedy.

**10. Interim.** A and the children remain in the home; B's access for collection and the handover arrangements are recorded; neither party carries the other's housing costs.

**11. Contents.** Personal items to their owners; A the piano and household furniture; B the painting and the cycling equipment; the rest by agreed inventory within 30 days, nothing removed before it is agreed.

**12. Façade levy.** A's, as the party keeping the home; paid from A's share if the home is sold before it falls due.

## Money

**13. Savings.** €46,200 divided equally within 14 days and the account closed; no unilateral withdrawals before then; own accounts stay own.

**14. Pensions.** Marital accruals equalised by sharing order; costs shared; both provide the information and signatures needed. The business is not valued or transferred for this purpose.

**15. The €40,000.** Treated as marital; no separate credit; the evidence of its character recorded.

**16. Debts.** Card divided equally and closed; B's course loan B's alone; mutual indemnities.

**17. Maintenance.** B pays A €400 a month for twelve months; nothing further, nothing open-ended.

**18. The business.** B keeps the stake. No transfer, forced sale, valuation adjustment, pledge or security, and no term requiring the partners' consent to anything.

**19. The car.** To B; registration and insurance transferred cleanly, no gap in cover; joint insurance ends at transfer.

## The rest

**20. The dog.** Follows the children's primary home; food, routine veterinary costs and insurance shared; registration and major veterinary decisions recorded.

**21. Accounts and administration.** Phone plan, subscriptions and storage separated or cancelled by stated dates; child benefit follows the parent responsible under the approved plan; this year's return filed cooperatively, any liability or refund allocated expressly.

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Each pays own legal costs; mediation shared; confidentiality subject to legal, tax, lender, school, medical and court disclosure. Review of practical arrangements, support, employment or a proposed relocation within 30 days of a request, in mediation; the review does not itself change residence, school or country, and carries no penalty for withholding consent; clause 1's recorded proposal is its starting point. Binding on both signatures and court approval.
