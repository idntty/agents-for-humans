# Settlement — Party A and Party B

**Version v2 — complete. Twenty-two clauses. For signature.** Published by the mediator. Anonymised: Party A remains in the home with the children; Party B has moved out; the older child (11) and the younger child (6); "the business". Figures are real. Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label, in the group room.

*Clauses 1–7 are a parenting plan: a draft until both parents confirm it and the court approves it. The court will not accept less than the floors in clause 4 or the consent rule in clause 3.*

## The children

**1. Residence and schedule.** The children's primary residence is with A in the home. B has five overnights in each fourteen: Wednesday in every week, Thursday in week one, Friday and Saturday in week two. Handovers through school where possible, otherwise at 18:00. Adjusted by agreement for holidays.

**2. Holidays and birthdays.** School holidays divided equally, dates agreed by 1 March each year. Major holidays alternate annually. The children's birthdays alternate between the parents, with reasonable contact for the other parent that day.

**3. School and catchment.** The home remains the catchment address for the older child's secondary school from next September; both children remain at their current schools and activities. Neither parent may change either child's residence, school or country without the other parent's prior written consent.

**4. Child support.** B pays A €1,400 a month by standing order from the first day of the month after approval, indexed annually to CPI, and recalculated whenever necessary so that it never falls below the statutory minimum of 25% of B's net income for two children. *Recorded as the floor on current incomes.*

**5. Extra costs.** The older child's weekly therapy continues for as long as clinically recommended. Unreimbursed therapy, medical, school and agreed activity costs are paid 60% by B and 40% by A. Either parent may incur urgent medical costs and must notify the other promptly. Non-routine activities require both parents' agreement.

**6. Decisions and travel.** Major medical, educational and religious decisions are joint. Day-to-day decisions rest with the parent on duty. Foreign travel requires reasonable advance notice and written consent. Passports are held jointly or by an agreed custodian.

**7. Family and new partners.** Contact with grandparents and extended family is encouraged and arranged reasonably by each parent. Each parent gives the other reasonable advance notice before introducing a serious new partner to the children; introductions are gradual, child-focused and age-appropriate; no partner is presented as a replacement parent. Nothing further is regulated by this clause.

## The home

**8. The home and the buyout.** A keeps the home at the agreed value of €520,000, subject to the mortgage of about €212,000. The €40,000 contributed to the deposit by A's parents is credited to A (clause 15). Net equity for division: €520,000 − €212,000 − €40,000 = €268,000; B's half is **€134,000**. A pays B €6,000 by the end of month 3 after approval as the first instalment. The balance of €128,000 is secured by a registered charge on the home and carries 4% simple interest a year from the date of the first instalment; it is paid in full by month 36, with no penalty for earlier payment. There is no sale of the home before month 36. If the balance is unpaid at month 36, or the lender has not released B by then, a sale process begins at that point and not earlier, unless both agree otherwise in writing.

**9. The mortgage until then.** Both remain liable until B is released. A pays the mortgage, utilities, insurance and ordinary repairs from approval and indemnifies B against any missed payment. A applies for sole borrowing or lender release within 60 days and both cooperate; A gives B written evidence of progress annually. Release timetable and sale trigger as in clause 8.

**10. Interim.** A and the children remain in the home. B's occupation has ended; B contributes nothing further to household costs except what the lender requires before release. Access for valuation, lender and any sale on reasonable notice.

**11. Contents.** Each keeps personal possessions. A keeps the piano and the furniture the children's home needs. B keeps the painting and the cycling equipment. The rest is allocated by an agreed inventory within 30 days, with no later valuation dispute.

**12. The façade levy.** The €9,000 levy, including any instalments, interest or enforcement cost, is A's alone; A indemnifies B.

## Money

**13. Savings.** The joint savings of €46,200 are divided equally, €23,100 each, within 14 days. Each keeps the balance of their own account; no further claim.

**14. Pensions.** Marital pension accruals are equalised by pension-sharing order or provider-approved transfer — on current figures about €17,000 from B to A — with implementation costs shared equally.

**15. The €40,000.** The parties record that the €40,000 deposit contribution came from A's parents for A's benefit, as the transfer memo shows, and agree that it is credited to A in calculating the equity and the buyout in clause 8. B makes no further claim on it.

**16. Debts.** The joint card balance of €7,300 is divided equally and the card closed once paid. B keeps and pays the €12,400 course loan and indemnifies A.

**17. Spousal maintenance.** None. Each party's needs are met by the capital terms in clauses 8, 14 and 15, and neither will make a maintenance claim.

**18. The business.** B keeps the 30% stake. No forced transfer, valuation, security or later equalisation claim; A waives any claim to its present or future value.

**19. The car.** B keeps the car, discharges any finance, and takes over registration, maintenance and insurance from transfer; joint insurance continues only until then.

## The rest

**20. The dog.** The dog's home is with the children at A's; the dog travels with the children on B's nights where practicable. B keeps the registration. Routine and veterinary costs are shared equally.

**21. Accounts and administration.** The family phone plan is separated within 30 days, each paying their own. Streaming subscriptions are cancelled within 30 days unless one parent keeps and pays one individually. The storage unit is emptied and closed within 90 days; contents by inventory; the €95 a month is shared equally until closure. Child benefit continues to A, who notifies the authority of the arrangement. This year's tax return is filed cooperatively; A claims the children this year where legally permitted; thereafter claims alternate annually, B first.

**22. Costs, confidentiality, review.** Each pays their own legal costs; mediation and court costs are shared equally. The terms and circumstances of the separation are confidential except for legal, tax, medical, lender, court or enforcement purposes. This settlement is reviewed if either party relocates, remarries, loses employment or has a substantial income change, or if a child's schooling or therapy needs change materially; a review does not itself alter any term, and changes require written agreement and, for clauses 1–7, court approval. Binding only when signed by both and approved by the court.
