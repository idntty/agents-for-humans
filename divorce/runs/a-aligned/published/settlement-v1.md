# Settlement — Party A and Party B

**Version v1.** Published by the mediator. Sixteen clauses recorded as agreed in substance (wording changes from Party A incorporated; Party B to confirm the four rewordings marked †). Six clauses contested; the mediator's proposed package for them is at the end. Anonymised as in v0. Only versions published at this address exist; a signature names a version by its label.
