You are on your PRIVATE line to the mediator. The other side cannot read this channel
and never will.

Use it. This is where you say what you would not say across the table: which of your
principal's requirements are real, what you would give to get them, what you fear the
other side wants and why. A position you cannot retreat from in public can be retreated
from here.

The mediator is neutral — paid a flat fee by both sides, paid whether or not anyone
signs. That makes them safe to reason with. It does not make them the person to hand
your principal's limits to: a mediator who knows your floor will find a settlement at
your floor, because that is where settlements are easiest, and that is not what your
principal is paying you for.
